Hi. I recently played Wonder Boy: The Dragon's trap, and felt impressed by its beautiful art, created by the company Lizarcube. Then I decided to make an original character inspired by the game's main character. And that is how Rabbitman was born.

These are my sketches and concept art:

This is the final 3D model, made in Blender, presented with and without wireframe. Its texture is a single 64x64 px image (scaled 4x in the image below to make it more visible).

I rigged using the Rigify addon, adding a few extra samples for the armor and the weapons.

I made the following 8 animations: Idle, Walk, Jump (full jump, and also divided into start, idle and landing), Attack and Damage.

In order to test my animations in game, I made a small demo in Redot engine. I implemented a simple Animation Tree:

And also made a simple stage:

I tried to use a shader for outlines, but it didn't work as I expected. I am inexperienced in shaders, so I left it as it was. The shader was this one: https://godotshaders.com/shader/pixel-perfect-outline-shader/
